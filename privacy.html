Privacy Policy

MS-12 · Last updated: 7 August 2026

MS-12 does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

MS-12 は、個人情報を一切収集・保存・送信しません。

No data collection

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It does not ask you to create an account or to sign in. The developer receives no information about you or about how you use the app.

解析ツール、広告、トラッキング、サードパーティ製 SDK はいずれも組み込まれていません。アカウント登録も不要です。

Microphone

MS-12 is a sampler, so it asks for microphone access in order to record audio. That audio is processed and stored entirely on your device. It is never uploaded, never sent to the developer, and never shared with anyone else. You can revoke microphone access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

録音した音声は端末内でのみ処理・保存され、外部に送信されることはありません。マイクの許可は「設定」からいつでも取り消せます。

Your recordings and projects

Samples, patterns and projects you create are saved in the app's own folder on your device. They remain under your control and are accessible through the Files app. Deleting the app deletes this data. The developer has no access to it.

作成したサンプルやプロジェクトは端末内のアプリ領域に保存されます。開発者がアクセスすることはありません。

Purchases

MS-12 offers a single one-time in-app purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees or handles your payment details. To determine whether the full version is unlocked, the app asks Apple's StoreKit framework about your purchase status for this app; no personal information is involved, and this is the only network activity the app performs.

決済はすべて Apple が処理します。アプリが決済情報を扱うことはありません。購入状態の確認に StoreKit を使用しますが、これがアプリの行う唯一の通信です。

Apple may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous sales and download reports. These contain no information that identifies an individual customer. Apple's handling of your data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

MS-12 collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains no advertising and no external links to user-generated content.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If a future version of the app were to begin collecting data, that change would be described here before it takes effect.
