Frequently asked

What is included in the free version?
The full sound engine — nothing about the audio quality is reduced. You get Bank A (16 pads), one 1-bar pattern, two effects, and microphone sampling of up to 10 seconds per pad.

無料版でも音質の制限は一切ありません。Bank A（16パッド）、1小節のパターン1つ、エフェクト2種、パッドあたり10秒までのマイクサンプリングが使えます。

What does the purchase unlock?
One purchase unlocks everything: all four banks (64 pads), unlimited patterns of up to 4 bars, Song Mode, all 13 effects, unlimited sampling time, loading your own audio files, project save and load, and WAV export. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Anything you made before buying carries over.

買い切りです。購入前に作ったものはそのまま引き継がれます。

I bought the full version but the app still shows the trial.
Open any locked feature to bring up the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

ロックされた機能をタップして購入画面を開き、「Restore Purchase」を選んでください。購入時と同じ Apple アカウントでサインインしている必要があります。

Where are my recordings and projects stored?
On your device, inside the app's own folder. You can reach them from the Files app under On My iPhone → MS-12. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them, so copy anything you want to keep out of that folder first.

端末内にのみ保存されます。「ファイル」App の〈このiPhone内 → MS-12〉から取り出せます。アプリを削除すると消えるのでご注意ください。

The app is not recording from the microphone.
Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure MS-12 is enabled. If you are using a USB or Lightning audio interface, connect it before opening the sampling screen.

「設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ → マイク」で MS-12 が有効か確認してください。

How do I request a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

返金は Apple が対応します。上記のページからお手続きください。
